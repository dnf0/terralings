# Welcome to Terralings

**Master OpenTofu & Terraform through interactive, hands-on practice.**

Terralings is designed to teach you Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) from first principles. Instead of passively reading documentation, you solve small, focused exercises directly in your editor.

---

### Core Philosophy

1. **Deterministic & Fast Feedback:** All exercises run locally in isolated sandboxes.
2. **Zero Cloud Friction:** You don't need AWS, Azure, or GCP credentials to learn core HCL syntax, resource schemas, variables, and module composition.
3. **True Compiler Feedback:** Exercises are evaluated directly by your installed OpenTofu or Terraform binary. Real syntax errors and validation messages guide your learning.
4. **Rustlings / Ziglings Model:** Clean exercise files with `# TODO:` markers—no magical comment manipulation required.
